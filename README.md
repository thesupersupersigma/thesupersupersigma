## About

I'm a self-taught developer who learns by building real things and keeping them running. I hand-code and maintain my own websites end to end — writing the HTML/CSS, handling deployment on Vercel, managing DNS and email, and migrating services when something needs to move. Outside of the browser I spend a lot of time in the terminal: administering a small Linux cloud server, running things in Docker, self-hosting tooling, and automating repetitive tasks with Python and shell scripts.

I'm comfortable across macOS, Linux, and Windows, and I enjoy the messy parts — reinstalls, recovery, drivers, partitions, the stuff that breaks. Right now I'm working through a full-stack path to fill in the fundamentals properly rather than just by trial and error.

**Open To**
- Open-source collaboration
- Learning opportunities and mentorship
- Freelance / internship work
- Connecting with other developers
